Your route down through a Java survival world — from first punch to the End and beyond. Click a rung to see what it takes.

SURFACE
First Night Shelter
In progress

Survive until dawn without dying. Doesn't need to be pretty.

  • Punch 3-4 logs minimum
  • Craft a crafting table + wooden pickaxe/axe/sword
  • Mine at least 5 cobblestone for a stone pickaxe
  • Wall off, dig into a hillside, or find a natural enclosure
  • Light the area (torches) so nothing spawns near you
Stone Age Toolkit
Locked

Upgrade off wood before it slows you down.

  • Full stone tool set (pickaxe, axe, shovel, sword)
  • Basic furnace running, start smelting cobblestone/food
  • Kill a few sheep/cows for wool (bed) and leather
  • Craft a bed and set your spawn point
Early Food Source
Locked

Stop relying on whatever you find lying around before hunger becomes a problem.

  • Find and till a small farm plot near water
  • Plant wheat, carrots, or potatoes (whichever you found first)
  • Fence it off from mobs
  • Optional: pen in 2 cows/sheep to start breeding
IRON AGE
Iron Gearing Up
Locked

The point where dying stops being a constant threat.

  • Mine enough iron for a full tool + armor set
  • Smelt it all, craft iron pickaxe/sword/armor
  • Make a shield
  • Stock a few stacks of torches

Redstone Basics
Locked

Not required, but makes everything after this easier. Worth a short detour.

  • Mine redstone dust, understand basic circuits
  • Build a simple door or piston contraption
  • Set up hoppers + a chest for item sorting
Diamonds & Basic Enchanting
Locked

Requires iron gear. Unlocks proper mining and your first enchantments.

  • Strip-mine or cave-dive at the right Y-level for diamonds
  • Diamond pickaxe (needed for obsidian)
  • Mine obsidian for a portal (needs diamond pickaxe)
  • Build bookshelf + enchanting table setup
  • Enchant your main sword/pickaxe/armor
THE NETHER
Nether Portal & First Steps
Locked

Getting in safely matters more than what you do once you're there.

  • Build and light your portal in a safe spot
  • Bring fire resistance or a way to deal with lava/ghasts
  • Mark your portal's location so you can find it again
  • Scout cautiously — note nearby fortresses/bastions from a distance
Fortress Run & Blaze Farm
Locked

Blaze rods are the whole reason you're here — they unlock brewing and Eyes of Ender.

  • Find and clear a path into a Nether Fortress
  • Farm blaze rods (manually or via a simple spawner farm)
  • Grab wither skeleton skulls if going for a Wither later

Piglin Bartering & Netherite
Locked

Optional but strong — netherite is the real endgame gear upgrade.

  • Get gold for piglin bartering (gold farm or mining)
  • Barter for useful loot (ender pearls, obsidian, gravel)
  • Find ancient debris, smelt into netherite scrap
  • Upgrade diamond gear at a smithing table
THE END
End Prep: Eyes of Ender
Locked

Finding the stronghold is usually slower than the fight itself.

  • Craft enough Eyes of Ender (blaze powder + ender pearls)
  • Throw eyes to triangulate the stronghold
  • Find the portal room, don't activate it yet if not ready
Ender Dragon Fight
Locked

Bring more than you think you need — this is the first real boss fight.

  • Bring beds (for damage), arrows, and healing (golden apples/potions)
  • Destroy end crystals on obsidian towers first
  • Fight the dragon, grab the egg + exit portal
Elytra & Outer End Islands
Locked

Elytra changes how you move around the whole world from here on.

  • Bridge or find a way to the outer End islands
  • Find an End City and loot an Elytra from the item frame
  • Grab shulker boxes for portable storage
